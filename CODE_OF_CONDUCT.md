# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the DevPilot community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or circumstance.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, and accountability. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized attention, trolling, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through their published GitHub contact details. Maintainers will investigate fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
